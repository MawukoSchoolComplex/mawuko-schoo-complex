About Mawuko School Complex

Mawuko School Complex provides quality education for Pre-School, Primary and Junior High School students. Our goal is to create a safe and inspiring environment where learners develop academically, socially and morally. We are dedicated to providing quality education that nurtures creativity, critical thinking, and leadership skills in our students.

Our Mission

What must be done must be done well. Our mission is to provide a nurturing and stimulating learning environment that empowers students to reach their full potential academically, socially, and emotionally.

Our Vision

To become a leading school known for academic excellence, discipline and innovation.

Our School Houses

Mawuko School Complex encourages teamwork, discipline, leadership, and healthy competition through its four school houses. Every student belongs to a house and participates in academic, sporting, cultural, and social activities throughout the year.

🔴 Socrates House

Inspired by the great philosopher Socrates, this house promotes wisdom, integrity, and critical thinking.

🟢Archimedes House

Named after the brilliant mathematician and inventor, this house represents creativity, discovery, and innovation.

🟡Sir Isaac Newton House

Inspired by Sir Isaac Newton, this house symbolizes excellence, curiosity, and scientific achievement.

🔵Albert Einstein House

Named after Albert Einstein, this house encourages imagination, perseverance, and a passion for learning.

Our School Facilities

Mawuko School Complex provides a supportive learning environment with facilities designed to enhance academic excellence, creativity, and personal development.

🏫 Modern Classrooms

Spacious and comfortable classrooms that support effective teaching and learning.

💻 ICT Laboratory

A computer laboratory that helps students develop digital skills.

🔬 Science Laboratory

A practical learning space for science experiments and discoveries.

📚 Library

A resource centre that encourages reading and independent learning.

🌳 School Garden

A green learning area that promotes environmental awareness and practical agricultural knowledge.

🍴 Dining Hall

A comfortable dining area where students enjoy meals in a clean and organized environment.

🚌 Transport Facilities

Reliable transportation services that support safe movement of students to and from school.

⚽️ Sports Facilities

Facilities that promote teamwork, fitness, and sporting talents.

🏠 Dormitories

Comfortable accommodation facilities that provide students (boarders) with a safe and supportive environment for learning and personal growth.

🏥 Sick Bay

A health facility that provides basic medical care and support to ensure the wellbeing of students.

🛕 Temple

A peaceful place that supports spiritual growth, reflection, and character development within the school community.

🍽️ School Canteen

A clean environment where students can access healthy meals.
