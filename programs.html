Our Academic Programs

Quality learning from Primary to Junior High School.

Pre-School (Nursery & Kindergarten)

Our Pre-School program provides a nurturing environment for young learners, focusing on social, emotional, and cognitive development through play-based learning and structured activities.

Subjects Include:

  • Creativity
  • Numeracy
  • OWOP
  • Music and Dance
  • Poems and Rhymes

Primary School

Our Primary program provides a strong foundation in literacy, numeracy, science, and creativity. We help learners develop confidence, discipline, and a love for learning.

Subjects Include:

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • History
  • OwOP
  • Creative Arts
  • Religious and Moral Education
  • Physical Education

Junior High School (JHS)

Our JHS program prepares students for academic success and the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) through effective teaching and continuous assessment.

Subjects Include:

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Social Studies
  • Computing
  • French
  • Basic Design and Technology
  • Religious and Moral Education
  • Career Technology
  • Ghanian Language & culture
  • Physical Education

Beyond the Classroom

Students also participate in activities that build confidence, leadership, teamwork, and creativity.

  • Sports
  • Debates
  • Clubs
  • Cultural activities
