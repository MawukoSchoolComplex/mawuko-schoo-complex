Welcome to Mawuko School Complex

We are delighted to welcome you to Mawuko School Complex. Our school is committed to providing quality education in a safe, disciplined and supportive environment.

We aim to help every learner develop academically, socially, morally and physically while discovering their unique talents and abilities.

About Mawuko School Complex

Mawuko School Complex provides education at the Pre-School, Primary and Junior High School levels.

Our approach combines effective teaching, discipline, creativity, teamwork and character development to prepare learners for the future.

Admission Requirements

Parents and guardians interested in enrolling their children at Mawuko School Complex should complete the admission process and provide the required information.

Applicants may be required to provide:

  • Completed admission form
  • Previous school records where applicable
  • Recent passport-size photographs

Additional requirements may apply depending on the class or level of admission.

Our Core Values

  • Discipline – Encouraging responsible behaviour.
  • Respect – Treating everyone with dignity.
  • Excellence – Striving to achieve our best.
  • Integrity – Doing what is right.
  • Teamwork – Working together to achieve goals.
  • Creativity – Encouraging ideas and innovation.

School Rules & Regulations

  • Learners should attend school regularly and punctually.
  • Students must respect teachers, staff and fellow learners.
  • School uniforms should be worn appropriately.
  • Learners should take good care of school property.
  • Bullying and other forms of misconduct are not permitted.
  • Parents and guardians are encouraged to work closely with the school.
